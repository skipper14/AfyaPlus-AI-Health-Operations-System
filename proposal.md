# Integrated AfyaPlus AI Health-Operations System

### Path Choice
The Official Recommended Path: The Integrated AfyaPlus Module 4 Capstone. This architecture coordinates the Week 6 Triage layer, the Week 7 cost-optimized logistics routing tools, the Week 8 explicit versioning matrices, and the Week 9 security containment architecture.

### Problem Statement
Clinical centers in low-resource environments require fast medical triage processing alongside logistics inventory tracking without blowing past operational budgets, exposing sensitive patient Protected Health Information (PHI), or suffering prompt injection exploits.

### Dataset Scale & Integrity
* Target Corpus: `fixtures/triage_messages_sample.jsonl` containing 300 synthetic patient validation records.
* Zero-Scrape Safety Rule: No live commercial environments or actual public health networks were crawled.
* Isolation Boundary: Commercial product catalog schemas are completely separated from patient healthcare records.

### Project Milestones
1. Phase 1 (Core Engineering): Port and isolate Week 6 FastAPI architectures and Week 8 parameter tables.
2. Phase 2 (Hardening): Wire the pre-model injection filter blocks and token log redactors.
3. Phase 3 (Evaluation & CI): Standardize testing pyramids, performance profiles, and GitHub actions integrations.
4. Phase 4 (Reporting): Build financial optimization forecasts and present architectural blueprints.

### Out of Scope
* Direct execution of live cloud resource provisoners or real cluster networking setups.
* Autonomous medical diagnosis generation or unreviewed financial budget alterations.
* High-volume database storage tests (restricted strictly to hundreds of records for testing).
